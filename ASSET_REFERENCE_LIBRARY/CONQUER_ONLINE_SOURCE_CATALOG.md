# Conquer Online — Source & Reference Catalog

Purpose: keep the Clash of Legends art direction grounded in Conquer Online rather than mixing unrelated fantasy packs into the final game.

## Important distinction
Publicly viewable images, archived clients, and fan databases are useful research references; public availability does not itself grant redistribution rights. Do not copy proprietary Conquer Online art, client files, maps, textures, sprites, or extracted models into this repository unless their redistribution license/permission is explicitly established. Keep source links and provenance for every reference. Use newly created, visually coherent replacement assets where redistribution permission is not established.

## Primary visual and content references
- Official game guide and content index: https://co.99.com/guide/info/game_info.shtml
  - Items, weapons, armor, accessories, NPCs, maps, monsters, skills and class references.
- Official item index: https://www.conqueronline.com/guide/items/items.shtml
  - Headgear, armor, accessories, one-handed and two-handed weapons, throwing knives, bows, gems, talismans and other equipment.
- Official class guides:
  - Archer: https://www.conqueronline.com/guide/classes/archer.shtml
  - Oriental Assassin: https://co.99.com/guide/classes/assassin.shtml
- Community game database: https://conquerita.com/en/database
  - Monster, hunting-map, equipment and progression data; treat as server-specific reference, not universal canonical data.
- Classic Conquer database and guides: https://wiki.conqueronline.net/
  - Items, spells, class guides, interface and game-system references.
- Classic Conquer monster reference: https://classicconqueronline.net/monsters/
  - Monster names, levels, stats and map associations; verify version differences.
- Conquer Online Development Wiki: https://conquer-online.github.io/wiki/
  - Community documentation of client file formats, maps, item/monster data and resource structures. Use for understanding/version comparison; do not bypass encryption or access controls.

## Historical client preservation index
- TQ Digital Games Archive: https://tqarchive.org/
  - Indexes historical Conquer Online clients and patch archives, with many files hosted by Archive.org. The archive describes its scope as publicly and freely available official files. These are complete client/patch archives, not a separately licensed art pack. Check the specific file's provenance and terms before reuse.
- Open-source preservation client (code only; explicitly does not include game assets): https://github.com/deklol/Preservation-CO-Client
  - Its documentation describes the expected legacy client resource folders and states that game assets are not included.

## Asset collection and art-production priorities
1. Archer → Oriental Assassin progression: dual throwing knives first, bows and quivers second; preserve the same character identity across the transition.
2. All other classes: Trojan, Warrior, Ninja, Monk, Pirate, Dragon Warrior, Taoists and later classes as applicable to the selected Conquer version.
3. Complete equipment families: weapon tiers, armor, robes, helmets, accessories, garments, mounts, pets and upgrade appearances.
4. NPCs, monsters, bosses, pets and mounts.
5. Twin City, Phoenix Castle, Ape Mountain, Desert City, Bird Island, markets, dungeons and other selected-version maps; include terrain, buildings, props, portals and minimap layouts.
6. Skills, projectiles, spell effects, loot, item icons, UI panels, cursors and interface states.

## Required provenance record for every imported file
Record: source URL, original filename, author/owner if known, license or permission URL, retrieval date, intended use (reference vs shipped asset), and any modifications. If license/permission is unknown, classify it as **REFERENCE ONLY — DO NOT REDISTRIBUTE**.

## Consistency rule
Do not fill gaps with unrelated third-party fantasy packs as final in-game assets. Use those packs only when their license permits and only after reworking them into the established Conquer-inspired visual language. Final game assets should share one coherent palette, proportions, lighting, camera scale and animation style.
